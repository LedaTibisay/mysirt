Specifications & Documentation

MySirt is accompanied by a structured documentation framework covering the entire ecosystem, from its conceptual foundations to its technical architecture and operational aspects.

Rather than a single monolithic manual, the documentation is organized as a collection of focused documents, each dedicated to a specific aspect of the platform. Together, they provide a comprehensive understanding of the MySirt technology, its execution model, architectural principles, and technology transfer process.

Documentation Structure

  • Ecosystem overview and conceptual foundations
  • DSL design principles and language semantics
  • Model interpretation and execution principles
  • Architectural design and execution layers
  • Regeneration and extensibility concepts

DSL Reference

The public documentation introduces the design principles and conceptual structure of the MySirt DSL. It explains how domain knowledge is expressed through entities, roles, workflows, rules, and responsibilities without exposing the complete language specification.

The DSL acts as an executable specification layer rather than as descriptive configuration, providing the conceptual foundation of the Model Execution Engine.

Engine Specification

Documentation describes how structured models are interpreted, validated, and deterministically transformed into executable system structures.

Execution contracts between layers are defined to ensure architectural stability and predictable regeneration.

Technical Materials

The MySirt documentation framework includes technical, architectural, academic, and conceptual materials describing the evolution, design, and operational aspects of the platform.

  • Public technical documentation
  • Architectural reference documents
  • Academic and conceptual materials
  • Versioned technical documentation
  • Historical evolution notes

Extension & Maintainability

The generated systems are designed to remain readable, standards-compliant, and compatible with controlled extension.

Documentation includes boundaries between generated structures and custom integration layers.
